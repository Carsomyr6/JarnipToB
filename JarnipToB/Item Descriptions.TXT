Bane of the Innocent +4
The first victims of this sword were not warriors. They were pilgrims. Peasants. Beggars. Those who could not fight back.

Long ago, this forsaken blade belonged to an unnamed holy champion who was suddenly stripped of his deity's grace. Instead of seeking penance, he chose the path of vengeance. It is said that the slaughter of the followers of his former faith was so great that the screams of his victims became permanently imbued within the blade.

Bane of the Innocent strikes terror into the hearts of those who face it and does not tolerate the touch of the righteous. Because of unspeakable atrocities committed with it in the past, only those who follow the path of the antipaladin, or who have become fallen paladins, can wield it.

STATISTICS:

Equipped abilities:
- Fire Resistance: +20%
- Characters of Good or Neutral alignment are slain upon equipping this sword

Combat abilities:
- Touch of Terror: 25% chance on hit to cause the target to panic for 4 rounds
- Dooming Presence: 5% chance on hit that all enemies within a 15-ft. radius panic and receive a -2 penalty to Armor Class, hit rolls, and damage rolls for 1 turn

THAC0: +4 
Damage: 2d4+4 (slashing), +2d4 (fire)
Speed Factor: 4 
Proficiency Type: Bastard Sword
Type: One-handed
Requires:
 10 Strength

Weight: 3

Price: 25,000 gp

The Triumphant +5
According to legend, The Triumphant first belonged to a barbarian king of Narfell who perished while defending his homeland from an invading horde of demons from the Endless Wastes. The sword was said to have been found on the battlefield after its wielder had single-handedly defeated an entire army. No body was ever found. Only the sword remained, lying amid the dead.

Since that time, The Triumphant has appeared in countless sagas of the northern barbarian tribes, becoming a powerful symbol of might and perseverance. Some say that whoever claims the sword is destined for greatness, while others believe that the blade simply finds those who are worthy of it.

Whatever the truth, those who take up The Triumphant soon discover that even impossible odds can be overcome.

STATISTICS:

Equipped Abilities:
- Strength: +1
- Constitution: +1

Combat Abilities:
- Heroic Inspiration: While enraged, increases Armor Class by 2, Critical Strike chance by 5%, and grants an extra attack per round
- Furious Strikes: Critical hits knock the target back and deal an additional 2d12 slashing damage

THAC0: +5
Damage: 1d10+5 (slashing)
Speed Factor: 5
Proficiency Type: Two-handed Sword
Type: Two-handed
Requires:
 16 Strength

Weight: 15

Price: 30,000 gp

Axe of the Worldbreaker +4
This mighty adamantine two-handed axe belonged to Blaiz the Worldbreaker, a giant warlord who once established a kingdom in the Spine of the World. After gathering an army of ogres, trolls, and orcs, Blaiz marched south, leaving a trail of destruction in his wake. His reign of terror finally ended when a band of adventurers from Luskan slew him in battle and claimed the axe as their prize.
