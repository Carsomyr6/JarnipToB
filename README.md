# Jarnip in ToB: A New Merchant in Saradush

MOD VERSION: v0.0.1 *(Design Phase)*

Introduction

*This project is currently under development.*

This mod brings **Jarnip**, the bonus merchant introduced in my previous mod, into the *Throne of Bhaal* campaign. Trapped within the besieged city of Saradush, Jarnip is desperate to escape and is willing to part with some of the greatest treasures from his private collection to raise enough gold to bribe the city guards.

The mod introduces six new legendary, artifact-quality weapons designed for the epic scale of *Throne of Bhaal*. Each weapon is intended to reinforce a distinct class fantasy while remaining balanced and faithful to the design philosophy of the original game, providing equipment worthy of a Bhaalspawn on the path to ascension.

Planned Features

* Adds Jarnip as a new merchant in Saradush.
* Introduces six new legendary weapons.
* Designed to integrate seamlessly into a vanilla *Baldur's Gate II: Throne of Bhaal* playthrough.
* Emphasizes unique gameplay identities rather than raw statistical power.
